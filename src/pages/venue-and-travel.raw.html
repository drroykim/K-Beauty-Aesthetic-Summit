Venue & Travel

Join us in the heart of Koreatown, Los Angeles, for an immersive educational experience in a modern, sophisticated setting.

📍 KOREATOWN, LOS ANGELES, CA

Summit Venue

Ethos Society

📍 3435 Wilshire Blvd, 14th Floor
Los Angeles, CA 90010

Located in the heart of Koreatown, Ethos Society offers a modern, high-energy environment for the summit. The space features floor-to-ceiling windows with panoramic views of Los Angeles, providing a sophisticated backdrop for our educational sessions and exhibitor showcases.

🅿️ Parking Information

Convenient parking is available in the on-site building garage for only $5 all day. Please note that the parking system requires your vehicle's license plate number for validation, so we recommend taking a photo of your plate before heading up to the 14th floor.

OFFICIAL PARTNER HOTEL

The Line LA

Your Home Base for the Summit

📍 3515 Wilshire Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90010

Just a 4-minute walk from Ethos Society — The Line LA is our official partner hotel and captures the modern, glamorous vibe of Koreatown, serving as a central hub for our attendees.

The Experience

Enjoy a cool, high-design atmosphere featuring a rooftop terrace garden and a celebrated on-site restaurant. The Line LA offers the perfect blend of style and comfort for discerning aesthetic professionals.

Networking Cocktail Party

The Line LA will host our official Saturday evening networking reception, allowing you to connect with faculty and colleagues in a relaxed, stylish setting without leaving your hotel.

Special Rates for Attendees

We have secured a block of rooms at a special rate exclusively for summit attendees. We encourage you to book early, as these discounted rates are limited and will be available only until the block is filled.

Airports & Transportation

For those traveling from out of state or internationally, there are two primary airport options:

✈️

Los Angeles International Airport

LAX

The closest major international gateway to Koreatown. Approximately 30-45 minutes to the venue depending on traffic. Multiple transportation options available including ride-share, taxi, and rental cars.

🛬

Hollywood Burbank Airport

BUR

A convenient alternative for domestic travelers, often offering a quicker arrival and departure experience. Approximately 20-30 minutes to Koreatown with lighter traffic and easier navigation.
